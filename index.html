01. Problem & Scope

Key Challenges in Modern Healthcare

Inaccessible Triage

Patients struggle to evaluate symptom severity, overloading ERs with non-emergencies while delaying urgent care.

Emergency Delays

Fragmented dispatch systems fail to simultaneously inform emergency contacts and nearby ambulance networks.

Post-Discharge Gaps

Lack of daily vital tracking and medication safety alerts leads to high 30-day readmission rates.
